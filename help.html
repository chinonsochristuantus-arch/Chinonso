Popular questions

How long does delivery take?

Most orders are delivered within 2–5 business days depending on your location and product size. You will receive a tracking update once your order is dispatched.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes. Richie Store offers cash-on-delivery for eligible orders. Please confirm that your address and phone number are correct before checkout.

What is your return policy?

Items can be returned within 7 days if they are damaged, incorrect, or not as described. Please contact customer care with your order number and product details.

How do I track my order?

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a confirmation message with tracking details. You can also contact our team to help you locate the status.

Can I change my order after checkout?

Order changes may be possible before dispatch. Send a quick message to support with your order number and the details you want to update.
